The 'Aegis' Villager Wristband

Real-Time Personal Safety

The Aegis wristband is a vital personal safety device for villagers in high-risk zones. It provides one-tap SOS functionality and monitors biometric data, sending real-time updates to the central dashboard, ensuring no one is left behind during an evacuation.

The 'Jatayu' IoT Sensor Pod

Guardian of the Hills

The 'Jatayu' is a rugged, all-weather, solar-powered IoT device forming the backbone of our data collection network. Deployed across Uttarakhand, these pods provide continuous, hyperlocal environmental data.

Technical Specifications:

  • Connectivity: LoRaWAN, 4G Fallback
  • Power: 20W Solar Panel & Li-ion Battery
  • Sensors: Rain Gauge, Hygrometer, Barometer
  • Enclosure: IP67 Weatherproof
  • On-Chain Logging: All data packets signed & logged

Multi-Channel Alert System

When a high-risk event is detected, alerts are instantly disseminated through multiple channels to ensure maximum reach, from government bodies to individual citizens.

Community SMS Alerts

Broadcasts are sent to all registered mobile numbers in the predicted impact zone with clear evacuation instructions in local languages.

Public Siren System

Integration with public announcement systems and sirens in vulnerable villages and towns, triggered automatically by the AI.

SDRF & Authority Portal

A dedicated, high-priority channel delivers detailed event data, predicted paths, and resource requirements directly to the State Disaster Response Force.
